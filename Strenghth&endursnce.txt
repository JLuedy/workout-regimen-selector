Week 1: Strength Focus
Goal: Lift heavier weights at lower reps (5–8 range) with 2 minutes of rest to maximize absolute strength.
• Day 1 (Monday) – Upper Body A
	• Barbell Bench Press: 4 sets × 5–6 reps
	• Bent-Over Barbell Rows: 4 sets × 6–8 reps
	• Overhead Barbell Press: 3 sets × 6–8 reps
	• Lat Pulldowns: 3 sets × 8–10 reps
• Day 2 (Tuesday) – Lower Body A
	• Barbell Back Squats: 4 sets × 5–6 reps
	• Romanian Deadlifts: 4 sets × 6–8 reps
	• Walking Lunges: 3 sets × 10 reps per leg
	• Planks: 3 sets × 45-second hold
• Day 3 (Wednesday) – Rest / Active Recovery (Light walking or mobility work)
• Day 4 (Thursday) – Upper Body B
	• Weighted Pull-Ups (or Assisted): 4 sets × 6–8 reps
	• Incline Dumbbell Press: 4 sets × 6–8 reps
	• Seated Cable Rows: 3 sets × 8–10 reps
	• Lateral Raises & Tricep Pushdowns (Superset): 3 sets × 10–12 reps
• Day 5 (Friday) – Lower Body B
	• Trap Bar Deadlifts: 4 sets × 5–6 reps
	• Bulgarian Split Squats: 3 sets × 8 reps per leg
	• Leg Press: 3 sets × 8–10 reps
	• Standing Calf Raises: 3 sets × 12–15 reps
• Days 6 & 7 (Saturday & Sunday) – Rest / Light Cardio (20–30 mins moderate jogging or cycling)
Week 2: Endurance & Conditioning Focus
Goal: Maintain strength loads using moderate weights, higher repetitions (10–15 range), supersets, and short rest periods (60 seconds) to build muscular endurance.
• Day 8 (Monday) – Upper Body Circuit
	• Dumbbell Thrusters: 4 sets × 10–12 reps
	• Push-Ups to Renegade Rows (Superset): 4 sets × 10 reps
	• Half-Kneeling Overhead Dumbbell Press: 3 sets × 12 reps
	• Resistance Band Pull-Aparts: 3 sets × 20 reps
• Day 9 (Tuesday) – Lower Body Endurance
	• Goblet Squats: 4 sets × 12–15 reps
	• Kettlebell Swings: 4 sets × 15–20 reps
	• Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts: 3 sets × 12 reps
	• Jump Squats (Bodyweight): 3 sets × 10 explosive reps
• Day 10 (Wednesday) – Rest / Active Recovery
• Day 11 (Thursday) – Full Body Density (EMOM style)
	• Every Minute on the Minute for 20 minutes: Alternating between 8 Dumbbell Clean-and-Presses and 10 Pull-Ups/Bodyweight Rows.
• Day 12 (Friday) – Metabolic Conditioning
	• Burpees: 4 sets × 12 reps
	• Dumbbell Walking Lunges: 3 sets × 15 steps per leg
	• Mountain Climbers: 3 sets × 45 seconds
	• Plank Shoulder Taps: 3 sets × 1 minute
• Days 13 & 14 (Saturday & Sunday) – Outdoor Endurance (Longer hike, jog, or steady-state cardio session
