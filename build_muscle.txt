Workout Schedule
• Day 1 (e.g., Monday): Full-Body A (Focus on Squat/Push patterns)
• Days 2–3: Rest and recovery
• Day 4 (e.g., Thursday): Full-Body B (Focus on Hinge/Pull patterns)
• Days 5–7: Rest and recovery
Workout A: Lower Body Push & Upper Body Push/Pull
Perform 3 sets of 8–12 repetitions for most exercises, resting 2–3 minutes on heavy compound lifts.
• Goblet Squat or Barbell Back Squat: 3 sets x 6–8 reps (Quads, Glutes)
• Dumbbell or Barbell Bench Press: 3 sets x 8–10 reps (Chest, Triceps)
• Seated Cable Row or Chest-Supported Row: 3 sets x 8–12 reps (Back, Biceps)
• Overhead Dumbbell/Barbell Press: 3 sets x 8–10 reps (Shoulders)
• Plank or Deadbugs: 3 sets x 30-second hold / 10 reps per side (Core)
Workout B: Lower Body Hinge & Upper Body Pull/Push
Perform 3 sets of 8–12 repetitions, keeping 1 to 2 reps in reserve before failure.
• Barbell Deadlift or Romanian Deadlift: 3 sets x 5–8 reps (Hamstrings, Glutes, Lower Back)
• Lat Pulldown or Pull-Up: 3 sets x 8–10 reps (Lats, Upper Back)
• Bulgarian Split Squat or Leg Press: 3 sets x 8–12 reps per leg (Quads, Glutes)
• Cable Chest Press or Incline Dumbbell Press: 3 sets x 8–12 reps (Chest)
• Dumbbell Lateral Raises & Tricep Pushdowns: 3 sets x 10–12 reps (Shoulders, Arms)
Key Rules for Muscle Growth
• Progressive Overload: Gradually add a small amount of weight or an extra rep when an exercise feels easy.
• Nutrition: Eat a slight caloric surplus (5%–10%) and aim for around 0.8 to 1 gram of protein per pound of body weight daily.
• Proximity to Failure: Stop each set 1 to 2 repetitions shy of complete muscular failure to stimulate growth without excess fatigue.
