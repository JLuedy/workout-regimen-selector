Week 1: Foundation & Build
• Day 1 (Monday) - Full Body Strength: Squats, push-ups, dumbbell rows, and overhead presses (3 sets of 8–12 reps).
• Day 2 (Tuesday) - Steady-State Cardio: 30–40 minutes of brisk walking, jogging, or cycling at a moderate pace.
• Day 3 (Wednesday) - Active Recovery: Light stretching, yoga, or a casual walk.
• Day 4 (Thursday) - Upper/Lower Split (Upper): Bench press, lat pulldowns, shoulder press, and bicep/tricep supersets.
• Day 5 (Friday) - High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT): 20–25 minutes of high-effort intervals (e.g., 30 seconds sprint, 60 seconds rest).
• Day 6 (Saturday) - Full Body Strength: Deadlifts, lunges, incline dumbbell press, and core planks (3 sets of 8–12 reps).
• Day 7 (Sunday) - Complete Rest: Full rest day to let muscles repair.
Week 2: Intensity & Push
• Day 8 (Monday) - Full Body Strength: Increase weight slightly from Week 1 on squats, rows, and push-ups (progressive overload).
• Day 9 (Tuesday) - Steady-State Cardio: 40 minutes of steady incline treadmill walking or rowing.
• Day 10 (Wednesday) - Active Recovery: Core mobility work or light walking.
• Day 11 (Thursday) - Upper/Lower Split (Lower): Romanian deadlifts, leg press, calf raises, and weighted core work.
• Day 12 (Friday) - HIIT & Conditioning: 25 minutes of kettlebell swings and bodyweight circuit intervals.
• Day 13 (Saturday) - Full Body Strength: Dumbbell thrusters, pull-ups (or assisted), and farmer's carries.
• Day 14 (Sunday) - Complete Rest: Total physical and mental recovery.
Key Fat Loss Rules
• Calorie Deficit: Eat slightly fewer calories than you burn (roughly a 300–500 calorie daily drop).
• Protein Intake: Eat high-protein foods (chicken, fish, tofu, eggs) to protect muscle mass while losing fat.
• Hydration & Sleep: Drink plenty of water and aim for 7–8 hours of quality sleep nightly.
